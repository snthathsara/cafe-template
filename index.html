Our Philosophy & Daily Craft

Rooted in culinary craft, slow mornings, and warm neighborhood hospitality.

Welcome to your neighborhood sanctuary. Every morning begins before dawn with our bakers hand-kneading flaky butter croissants, and our baristas dialing in single-origin espressos. From seasonal wild mushroom tartines to fragrant pour-overs and Basque burnt cheesecake, we are dedicated to wholesome ingredients and artisanal technique in every cup and plate.
